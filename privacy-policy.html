Privacy Policy — Faro

Effective date: 8 June 2026

App: Faro (com.androiddev.khalid.oneplatform)

Contact: iamandroid.eng@gmail.com

Faro works entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal or health information. There is no account and no server.

What Faro stores, and where

  • Medications, reminders, and history you enter are stored only on your device, in an encrypted database. They are never uploaded.
  • Camera use (magnifier and read-aloud): images are processed on your device to magnify or recognise text. They are not saved and not transmitted.
  • Pill photos you optionally add are stored in the app's private storage on the device only.
  • App settings (text size, theme, reading speed) are stored locally.

What we do not do

  • No account, login, or personal profile.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No background location.

Permissions and why

  • Camera — magnifier and read-aloud (on-device only).
  • Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alerts — to deliver medication and reminder alerts reliably and on time.
  • Run after restart — to restore your reminders after the phone reboots.

Your control and deletion

You can edit or delete any medicine or reminder in the app at any time. Uninstalling Faro permanently removes all of its local data. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is no remote data to request or delete.

Children

Faro is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

Medical disclaimer

Faro provides reminders and assistance only. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, check drug interactions, or provide dosing guidance. Always follow your doctor and pharmacist.

Changes

If a future version adds optional features that transmit data (for example, an opt-in caregiver sync), this policy will be updated and that feature will be off by default and require explicit consent.
