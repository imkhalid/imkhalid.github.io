Block Blitz — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 11, 2026

Block Blitz ("the app", "the game") is developed and published by Khalid Saeed ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. Block Blitz does not require an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your scores, coins, unlocked themes, level progress, win streak, and settings (music, sound, board size) are saved locally on your device and are not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

2. Information used for online battles and friends

When you use Online Battle or Friends features, the game stores the following in Google Firebase Realtime Database so matches and friend lists can work:

  • A device identifier (Android ID) used to tell players apart
  • An automatically generated player name (for example "Player123")
  • Your randomly assigned 6-digit Friend ID and your friends list
  • Your score during a live match, and an online/offline indicator while the game is open

This data identifies your device, not you personally. It is processed by Google Firebase — see the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation. If you never use online features, none of this data is created.

3. Advertising

Block Blitz shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the game free. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from AdMob. Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), you will be shown a consent message and can choose non-personalized ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

4. What we do NOT collect

  • No names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • No contacts, photos, files, or messages
  • No precise location
  • No payment information (the game has no in-app purchases)

5. Children

Block Blitz is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the online features, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Online battle rooms are deleted automatically when a match ends or a player disconnects. Friend IDs, player names, and friends lists remain stored so your friends keep working between sessions. To have this data deleted, email us your 6-digit Friend ID (shown in the game under Battle → Friends) at the address below, and we will remove it within 30 days.

7. Security

Online data is transmitted over encrypted connections to Google Firebase servers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we only store the minimal data needed for gameplay.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

9. Contact
