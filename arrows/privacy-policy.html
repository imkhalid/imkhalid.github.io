Privacy Policy

Arrow Knots (Android) · Effective 20 July 2026

This policy describes what information the Arrow Knots app (package com.androiddev.khalid.arrows) handles, and how. The short version: the game itself collects no personal information and runs fully offline; the only data leaving your device is what our advertising provider (Google AdMob) collects to serve ads.

Information we collect ourselves

None. Arrow Knots has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own.

The game stores the following locally on your device only, and it never leaves it:

  • Your level progress, daily-challenge streak, and trophy totals
  • Your settings (sound and vibration preferences)

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. We cannot access it, and consequently we cannot delete it for you remotely — there is nothing on our side to delete.

Shared puzzles

When you share a puzzle from Shape Studio, the app creates a short text code that encodes only the shape you drew and a random number used to build the puzzle. It contains no personal information. Sharing happens through your device's standard share menu — we never see or store the codes you share, and codes work entirely offline.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Arrow Knots shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party service by Google. Like most mobile advertising services, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising ID)
  • Approximate device information (model, OS version, language, coarse location inferred from IP)
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped)

This collection is governed by Google's own policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob.

Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the UK, and other regions requiring consent, the app shows Google's consent form before serving personalized ads, and you can decline personalization. On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet / network state — used only to load ads. The game plays fully offline.
  • Vibration — for gameplay haptics; can be turned off in Settings.

Children

Arrow Knots is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — including children — and ad serving is handled by Google under the policies linked above. If you believe a child has provided personal data through an ad interaction, please contact Google via the links above, or us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change (for example, adding cloud saves or analytics), this page will be updated before the change ships, with a new effective date at the top.
