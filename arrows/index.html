How it plays

Tap an arrow and it slides out along its winding body — head first, tail following every bend. A clear path means escape; a blocked one costs a heart. Clear the whole knot without losing all three. No timers, no move limits, guaranteed solvable.

● Draw your own puzzles

Sketch any shape in Shape Studio — a heart, a letter, anything — and it instantly becomes a solvable puzzle you can share as a short code. No account needed.

● A new knot every day

One daily challenge, identical for every player in the world. Keep the streak alive and collect trophies as your totals grow.

● Relaxing by design

Black ink on soft paper, gentle sounds, nothing flashing. Play offline, anywhere, at your own pace.

● Hundreds of levels

From gentle first knots to dense winding mazes. Every level is a picture — clear it arrow by arrow and watch the shape dissolve.
